# DeshiPay · Instagram/Facebook carousel (5 × 1080px)

## Caption

Somebody wanted to give you money today. Then they had to ask how.

That one question is where a lot of donations, tips and small sales quietly end. Four numbers pasted into a caption, a screenshot sent twice, and a gateway that wants upfront and renewal fees before you have received a single taka.

DeshiPay is one page with your Bangla QR, wallet numbers and bank details. People pay from their own bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank app, straight to you. We take no commission, no cut, and we never hold the money. The code is open source, the page is free to create, and the payment happens in the customer’s own app. Normal payment-app charges may still apply.

Make your page free at deshipay.netlify.app and put the link where people already ask.
