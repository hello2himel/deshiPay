Contact & FAQ

Last updated: 2 November 2026

A small independent project — messages go straight to the maintainer. These are the questions people ask most; if yours is different, mail us.

Is DeshiQR a payment gateway?

No. It builds and hosts a page that shows your QR and generates an amount-bearing QR in the visitor’s browser. The payment itself happens in the customer’s bKash/Nagad/bank app, straight to the account in your QR. DeshiQR never receives or holds money.

Does the generated QR really work?

It is a standard EMV/Bangla QR payload with the amount inserted and the checksum re-sealed by the same engine that desktop tools use, so bank apps that support Bangla QR can normally scan it. Each bank decides what it accepts — always try one small payment yourself before sharing your link.

My QR won’t scan / the app rejects it

Check that the original QR is a Bangla QR from the same app family, and that the app’s version is up to date. If a specific app refuses amount-bearing QR codes, get in touch with the details and we’ll try to help. You can also scan the QR from the page to see exactly what it contains.

How do I create a page?

Open Create a page, sign up, drop a screenshot of your QR, choose a link and colour, and publish. If the database is paused, the same flow still works and gives you a standalone HTML file to host yourself.

How do I delete my page or account?

Sign in and use Delete my page in the dashboard. To delete the account and email as well, mail us from the address you signed up with. See the privacy policy for details.

Someone is using my name / my QR on a page

Mail us the page link and a screenshot. Pages that impersonate someone or use a QR they do not own are removed. Remember that anyone can put any name on a page, so customers should always check the merchant name inside their banking app before paying.
