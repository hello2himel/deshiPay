Payment pages for everyone

One link to receive payments, donations and support.

DeshiPay gives your business, work or cause a page that takes payments. Customers scan the live QR with the amount already in it, or copy your bKash, Nagad or bank details. No app to install. We never touch the money.

Create your page
  • Pay by QR or by details. A real, amount-ready Bangla QR leads the page, with wallet and bank details beside it for people who’d rather copy and send.
  • Get your own address: /yourname
  • For more than shops. Take a sale, an invoice, a tip or a donation. You choose the page, customers choose the amount.

How it works

  1. Add how you get paidA Bangla QR screenshot, wallet number or bank details is enough.
  2. Pick your linkChoose a name and a colour that feels like yours.
  3. Share itCustomers pay from their own app, for a purchase, a donation or support. You get paid as usual.
