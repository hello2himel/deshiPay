Questions people ask

Is DeshiPay a payment gateway?

No. DeshiPay builds and hosts the page that shows your QR and payment details. The payment happens in the customer's own bKash, Nagad or bank app. We never receive or hold money.

What can I use it for?

A shop taking a sale, a freelancer invoicing a client, a creator collecting tips, a cause taking donations, or anyone receiving support. The page works the same way.

Does the QR really work?

It is a standard Bangla QR with the amount inserted and the checksum re-sealed. Each bank decides what it accepts, so try one small payment yourself before sharing your link.

Do my customers need an app or an account?

No. They only need your link and a banking app they already use. You need an account to publish, or you can build the page offline and download it.

What does it cost?

No commission. Creating and sharing a page is free, and DeshiPay takes no cut from any payment. Your payment app's usual charges still apply; that money goes to them, not us.

Can I change my page later?

Yes. Sign in to edit your link, QR, wallet and bank details, theme and quick amounts, then publish again.
