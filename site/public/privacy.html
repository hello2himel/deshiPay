Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

DeshiPay is a small independent project operated by Himel Das. It gives people and businesses one page to receive payments, donations and support. This page explains what data the website stores, why, and what you can do about it. We collect the minimum needed to run a payment page.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password hash, managed by Supabase Auth. We never see your password. Signing up (and resetting a forgotten password) also sends a 6-digit one-time code to your email.
  • Your page: the page name (username), title, optional note, colour theme, quick-amount list, the amount-free base of the Bangla QR you uploaded, the merchant name and city read from that QR, and any wallet numbers or bank details you add.
  • Timestamps: when your page was created and last updated.
  • Technical logs: our hosts (Netlify, Supabase) keep standard request logs such as IP address and user agent under their own policies.

We do not collect payment data, card numbers, or transaction history. Payments happen in your customers’ own banking apps and never pass through DeshiPay. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we store it

To create and show your page, to sign you in, to keep the service working and secure, and to let you edit or delete your page. Your page data is deliberately public: anyone who visits your page sees the title, note, QR and payment details, because that is what a payment page is for.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase: authentication and the Postgres database.
  • Netlify: static hosting and the deploy pipeline.
  • Google Fonts: font files loaded by the browser (receives the visitor’s IP).
  • GitHub: source code and deploys.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell data or share it beyond what is needed to run the site.

Cookies and local storage

There are no tracking or advertising cookies. The browser stores your Supabase sign-in session, a draft of a page you are editing, and your light/dark theme choice. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

How long, and deleting it

Your page stays until you delete it from the dashboard (or turn it off to hide it). To delete your page, sign in and use Delete my page. To delete your account and email, email the address below and we will remove it. Hosting and database providers keep their own logs for their standard periods.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and the database uses row-level security: only you can change your row, and public pages are served through a limited function. The public “anon” key used by the browser is safe to expose by design; the service key is never shipped to the browser or stored in the repo. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password unique and report anything suspicious.

Children

DeshiPay is not intended for children. You must be at least 18 to create an account.

Your choices and contact

You can ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data, or object to how it is used, by writing to stargzr.science@gmail.com. We answer in good faith and as quickly as we can. If these terms change materially, the date at the top changes.

DeshiPay

Not a payment processor. DeshiPay is just a page that shows how to pay: your live QR, wallet and bank details. Customers pay from their own banking app, and we never receive or hold the money. No commission, no cut.
