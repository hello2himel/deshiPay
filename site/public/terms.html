What DeshiPay is — and is not

DeshiPay is an independent, open-source project that builds and hosts payment pages for shops, freelancers, creators and causes. A page can take a sale, an invoice, a tip, a donation or support — whichever lawful reason you use it for. DeshiPay is not a bank, a payment gateway, a money-transfer service or a merchant acquirer.

  • We never process, hold, receive or confirm payments.
  • Customers pay from their own banking or wallet app, directly to the account inside the QR you upload. No money passes through DeshiPay.
  • We do not verify merchants, accounts or QR codes, and we cannot guarantee that a particular bank or wallet app accepts a generated QR.
